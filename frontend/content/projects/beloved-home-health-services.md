## Overview

Beloved Home Health Services is a production-grade healthcare web platform built to establish a trusted digital presence for a licensed home health care provider located in Houston, Texas. The platform provides clear, accessible information for patients and families navigating skilled nursing, therapy services, and home health aide options.

## Architecture & Features

- **Modern Frontend Stack:** Engineered with Nuxt 4 and Vue 3, leveraging optimized server-side rendering for fast load times and clean accessibility.
- **Responsive Healthcare UI:** Styled using Tailwind CSS and NuxtUI to present core medical divisions including Registered Nursing (RN), Licensed Vocational Nursing (LVN), specialized physical/occupational therapies, and comprehensive personal care support.
- **Insurance & Payment Integration:** Features a structured breakdown of accepted public and private coverage options (including Medicare, Medicaid, and managed care plans) alongside clear patient intake guidance.
